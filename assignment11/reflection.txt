1. Static visualization are fixed images such as charts and graphs that do not allow user interaction, they are useful for
printed reports and presentations. Interactive visualization allow users  to explore data more deeply. You can zoom, hover, filter and change inputs.
2. Dashboards are signifficant advantage for realtime data exploring.  They enable users to monitor live metrics, identify trends quickly and respond
to changes without needing to generate new static charts.
3. Interactive tools like Plotly and Dash enhance data communication in professional settings by making complex data more accessible and understandable.
